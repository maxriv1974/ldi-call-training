New Client — First Contact

Goal: Make them feel heard, gather intake info, send the intake form, start the pipeline

1. Answer
2. Listen
3. Explore
4. Insurance
5. Next Step
6. Log & Send
💬 What You Say
"Thank you for calling Love Discovery Institute, this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?"
"Good [morning/afternoon], you've reached Love Discovery Institute — my name is [Name], how can I assist you?"
Tone: Warm, calm, unhurried. Many callers are nervous. Let silence breathe.
⚙️ What You Do Internally
GHL auto-logs the inbound call. No action needed yet. GHL
Pull up the GHL Conversations panel — check if this number is already in the system before the caller finishes their first sentence
If no contact record found → be ready to create one after the call
Note: call is being recorded if the number has recording enabled
💬 What You Say — Listen First
"I'm so glad you reached out. Tell me a little about what's bringing you in."
"Absolutely, I'd love to help. What's going on for you?"
Key: Do NOT interrupt. Let them share fully before you ask anything. Clients will tell you almost everything you need if you just listen for 30–60 seconds.
🔍 What You're Listening For
Who the therapy is for (self, partner, child, family)
Listen: "my husband and I…" / "I've been struggling…" / "my daughter…"
Urgency level — is this a slow consideration or an acute need?
Listen: "for a while now" vs. "things just fell apart last week"
Prior therapy experience
Listen: "I've tried therapy before but…" or "this is my first time"
Insurance hints
Listen: "I'm hoping you take my insurance" or "I'll pay out of pocket"
💬 What You Say
"Thank you for sharing that with me. I want to make sure we connect you with the right person — can I ask a few quick questions?"
"Are you looking for support for yourself, or for you and your partner, or for someone in your family?"
"Have you been in therapy before, or would this be your first time?"
"Do you have a preference for in-person sessions here in Coral Gables, or would telehealth work better for you?"
🔍 Exploration Questions
Who needs therapy?
Routes to correct therapist type
What are the main concerns you'd like to work on?
Informs therapist matching by specialization
Is there any preference for the therapist's gender or background?
Important for cultural/comfort fit
Do you need a Spanish-speaking therapist?
LDI serves a bilingual community
Any time-of-day preferences for appointments?
Increases show rate when schedule fits their life
⚙️ Internally — Taking Notes
Open GHL contact record (or start creating one)
Note: service type (Individual / Couples / Family) GHL
Note: presenting concern — anxiety, depression, relationship, trauma, etc.
Note: language preference, telehealth vs in-person, gender pref
Do NOT promise a specific therapist yet — check availability first
💬 Insurance Conversation
"Do you have health insurance you'd like to use for therapy, or are you planning to pay out of pocket?"
If YES: "Great — what insurance plan do you have? And do you happen to have your member ID available?"
If NO / Self-pay: "No problem at all. Our self-pay rate is [rate] per session. Does that work for you?"
"I want to make sure I give you accurate information — our billing team will verify your exact benefits and call you back within 24 to 48 hours. Does that work?"
⚙️ Internally
Collect: Plan name, Member ID, Group #, Date of Birth Required
Enter into GHL contact record GHL
Do NOT confirm or deny coverage yourself
After call: submit to Insurance Verification Form in GHL GHL
Move pipeline to Stage 3: Verify Insurance GHL
Tag contact with insurance plan name
💬 Setting the Next Step
"Here's what's going to happen next: I'm going to send you our intake form via text right now — it takes about 5 to 10 minutes to fill out, and once we receive it, we'll move forward with getting you scheduled."
"Our billing team will also verify your insurance and give you a call back, usually within 24 to 48 hours, to go over your benefits before your first session."
"Is there anything else you'd like to know or anything I can answer for you right now?"
"We're really glad you reached out — this is a great step. Take care, and you'll hear from us soon!"
⚙️ Closing the Call
Confirm intake form will go out immediately after call PracticeQ
Give your name so they know who they spoke with
Confirm best phone number and email for follow-up
After hanging up: send intake form link via GHL SMS GHL
Post-Call Checklist — complete within 5 minutes of hanging up
⚙️ GHL Actions
Create or update contact record with: name, phone, email, DOB GHL
Add call note: "Spoke with [Name]. Interested in [service type]. Insurance: [plan]. Sent intake form." GHL
Move opportunity to Stage 1: New Lead (or Stage 2 if they've been before)
Add tags: service type, insurance plan, language, therapist preference
Send intake form SMS via GHL workflow or manually
Submit insurance info to Insurance Verification Form
Set follow-up task: "Check if intake form received" — due in 48 hours
⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid
Confirming insurance coverage on the call — never do this
Promising a specific therapist before checking availability
Forgetting to send the intake form immediately
Not logging a note — other team members will follow up blind
Rushing the call — the first impression sets the tone for the entire therapeutic relationship

Couples Therapy Inquiry

Goal: Understand the couple's situation, set appropriate expectations, match to a couples-trained therapist

Couples calls require extra care. One or both partners may be calling. There may be conflict, ambivalence, or one partner dragging the other. Remain neutral and non-judgmental at all times.
1. Open
2. Explore
3. Align
4. Insurance
5. Next Steps
💬 Opening
"Thank you for calling Love Discovery Institute. I understand you're interested in couples therapy — is that right?"
"I'm happy to help. Are you calling on behalf of both of you, or just yourself right now?"
⚙️ Internally
Pull up or create contact in GHL — note "Couples" service type
Check which therapists are trained in couples work and have availability
💬 Exploration Scripts
"Can you tell me a little about what's been going on for the two of you?"
"How long have you and your partner been together?"
"Have the two of you been in couples therapy before?"
"Are you both interested in working with a therapist, or is one of you more hesitant?"
"Do you have a preference for in-person sessions, or would telehealth work better for both of you?"
🔍 What You're Exploring
Is this a crisis (recent affair, separation) or a slow burn (communication issues)?
Informs urgency and therapist matching
Are both partners bought in?
If one is reluctant, normalize it: "It's very common for one partner to feel unsure at first."
Prior couples therapy experience?
Helps set expectations; if they've tried before, explore what worked/didn't
Any specific concerns — affair, communication, intimacy, divorce consideration?
Matches to therapist with right specialization
Language preference for both partners?
Both need to be comfortable with the therapist
⚙️ Internally
Note all above in GHL contact record
Identify 1–2 couples-trained therapists who match their needs
Note: for couples, BOTH partners need to be on the intake form
Couples sessions are typically longer (50–60 min) and may have different rates
💬 Aligning on the Process
"In couples therapy, both partners meet together with the therapist — the focus is on the relationship, not on one person being 'the problem.'"
"Our therapists who specialize in couples work are trained in approaches like the Gottman Method and Emotionally Focused Therapy, which are very research-backed."
"The first session is typically a joint session where the therapist gets to know both of you. From there, the therapist will guide the structure of future sessions."
⚠️ Things to Watch For
One partner calling to "report" on the other — redirect gently: "In couples therapy, our therapist stays neutral for both of you."
Questions about confidentiality between partners — explain the therapist's policy upfront
Someone calling to book individual therapy to "fix the relationship" — clarify the difference
💬 Insurance for Couples
"Just so you know — couples therapy at Love Discovery Institute is self-pay. We don't bill insurance for couples sessions."
"Our couples session rate is [rate]. Would you like me to share more about what's included?"
⚙️ Couples Billing Notes
⚠ LDI does NOT accept insurance for couples therapy — self-pay only
Quote the self-pay rate for couples sessions upfront
Do NOT submit a couples case to insurance verification
Tag contact as "Self-Pay" in GHL regardless of individual insurance status
💬 Setting Next Steps
"I'm going to send you our intake form right now — there will be a section for each partner to complete. Once we have that, we'll get you scheduled with one of our couples therapists."
"We'll also have our billing team confirm your insurance benefits and follow up within 24 to 48 hours."
"I think you're going to find this really helpful. We work with a lot of couples and we're really proud of the work our therapists do. You're in good hands."
⚙️ Post-Call
Send couples intake form link — both partners need to complete
Add note: presenting concern, urgency, both partner details
Tag: "Couples", insurance plan(s), language preference
Move to pipeline Stage 1: New Lead

Insurance Question / Verification

Goal: Collect insurance info, set accurate expectations, escalate to billing team — never confirm coverage yourself

Rule #1: Never confirm or deny insurance coverage on the phone. Always say "let us verify and call you back."
1. Open
2. Collect Info
3. Set Expectations
4. Log & Escalate
💬 Opening the Insurance Conversation
"Absolutely, I'd be happy to look into that for you. Insurance can be a bit complicated, so I want to make sure we give you the most accurate information."
"Just so you know, I'm going to take down your insurance details and have our billing team verify your exact benefits — that way you won't have any surprises."
⚙️ Prepare
Pull up contact in GHL — check if insurance was already collected
Have insurance verification form ready to submit after the call
💬 Collecting Insurance Info
"What insurance plan do you have?"
"Do you have your insurance card available? I'll need your Member ID and Group Number."
"And what's the primary insuredperson's date of birth?"
"Are you the primary on the plan, or is someone else the policyholder?"
"And are you looking specifically for in-network coverage, or are you open to out-of-network benefits as well?"
🔍 What to Explore
In-network only, or open to OON?
Sets expectations before verification is complete
Has their deductible been met this year?
If not, they'll pay full contracted rate until deductible is met
Are they the primary policyholder or a dependent?
Billing needs primary's info; dependent has different ID
⚙️ Required Collection
Insurance plan / carrier name Required
Member ID number Required
Group number Required
Primary insured's date of birth Required
Relationship to policyholder (self, spouse, dependent)
Enter all into GHL contact record GHL
💬 Setting Expectations
"Our billing team will verify your benefits and give you a call back within 24 to 48 business hours. They'll go over exactly what's covered, what your co-pay or deductible looks like, and answer any specific questions."
If they push for an answer now: "I completely understand you want to know right away — I just want to make sure you have accurate numbers rather than a guess. Our billing team will have the real answer for you quickly, I promise."
"In the meantime, I can also send you our intake form so we're ready to book you as soon as insurance is confirmed. Would that be helpful?"
⚠️ What NOT to Say
"I think we take that insurance" — never guess
"You should be covered" — not your call to make
"It depends" without a follow-up plan — always close with a concrete next step
⚙️ Post-Call — Insurance
Log all insurance info in GHL contact record GHL
Submit Insurance Verification Form in GHL GHL
Move pipeline to Stage 3: Verify Insurance
Set follow-up task: "Insurance verification — call client back with results" due in 48 hrs
Add note with what was discussed and caller's questions
💬 When Billing Calls Back
[If covered + co-pay]: "Great news — we verified your benefits and we're in-network with your plan. Your co-pay will be $[X] per session."
[If unmet deductible]: "We're in-network with your plan, but your deductible hasn't been met yet. Until it's met, you'd pay $[X] per session, then your co-pay takes over."
[If not covered]: "We checked and unfortunately your plan doesn't cover mental health with our practice. Our self-pay rate is $[X] — would you like to move forward with that?"

No-Show Recovery Call

Goal: Check in with care, explore what happened, re-engage without pressure

Mindset: Lead with genuine concern, not accountability. "We missed you" not "you missed your appointment."
1. Open Warmly
2. Explore Why
3. Re-engage
4. Close
💬 Opening the No-Show Call
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] calling from Love Discovery Institute. I just wanted to reach out and check in — we missed you at your appointment today and wanted to make sure everything is okay."
If voicemail: "Hi [Name], this is [Name] from Love Discovery Institute. We missed you today and just want to make sure you're okay. Please give us a call back when you get a chance at [number]. We'd love to hear from you."
⚙️ Before the Call
Check PracticeQ — confirm it was a no-show, not a reschedule that got missed PracticeQ
Check GHL for any texts, emails, or notes explaining the absence GHL
Note how many no-shows this client has had previously
Call within same business day if possible; next morning at the latest
💬 Exploring What Happened
"Is everything okay on your end? I just want to check in."
"Did something come up that prevented you from making it?"
If cost concern: "I hear you — cost can definitely be a concern. Can I share a few options that might help?"
If ambivalent: "That's completely okay — a lot of people feel unsure at first. There's no pressure at all."
🔍 Listen for the Real Reason
Scheduling conflict / forgot
Easy re-book — offer to schedule now
Cost concern
Revisit insurance or payment options; check if billing was communicated clearly
Ambivalence about therapy
Normalize it; don't push. "Totally understandable. What would make you feel more comfortable?"
Personal crisis or emergency
Lead with empathy. Ask if they're okay first. Reschedule can wait.
Dissatisfied with therapist or prior session
Important feedback. Ask gently. Offer to discuss options with supervisor.
⚙️ Internally
Do not mention late cancel fee on the first call — lead with care
Note the reason in GHL GHL
If cost is the issue — flag for billing team
If dissatisfied — flag for clinical supervisor
💬 Re-Engaging
"Would you like to get rescheduled? I can look at availability right now if you have a moment."
"If now isn't a great time to schedule, I can send you a link so you can pick a time that works best for you."
"There's no pressure at all — whenever you're ready, we're here."
⚙️ If They Want to Reschedule
Check therapist availability in PracticeQ PracticeQ
Book the new appointment while on the call if possible
Send confirmation SMS via GHL GHL
If they prefer self-scheduling: send GHL booking link
💬 Closing the Call
[If rebooked]: "You're all set for [date/time]. You'll get a confirmation text shortly. We're looking forward to seeing you."
[If not ready]: "No problem at all. I'll send you our scheduling link and whenever you're ready, you can book directly. The door is always open."
[If total drop]: "I completely understand. I'll make a note and if anything changes, please don't hesitate to reach out. Take care of yourself."
⚙️ Post-Call Logging
Log reason for no-show and outcome of call in GHL GHL
If 3rd no-show: discuss with supervisor; consider moving to "Recent Drops"
Update PracticeQ appointment status: No-Show PracticeQ
If billing question raised: log for billing team follow-up

Goal: Efficiently match the client to the right appointment while confirming all prerequisites are met

1. Pull Up Record
2. New vs. Existing
3. Book
4. Confirm
💬 Opening
"Of course! Let me pull up your account. Can I get your name and the best phone number on file?"
⚙️ Before Anything Else
Search GHL by name or phone number GHL
Open PracticeQ record and check appointment history PracticeQ
Confirm: intake form on file? Insurance verified? Therapist assigned?
💬 Checking Status
[New client]: "I see you're new with us — before I book you, I just want to confirm we received your intake form. Have you had a chance to fill that out?"
[Existing client]: "I can see you've been working with [Therapist Name]. Are you looking to schedule your next regular session, or is this something different?"
[Reschedule]: "No problem at all. I can move that — when does your schedule open up?"
[Cancel]: "I'm sorry you need to cancel. Can I ask if you'd like to reschedule at the same time, or are you not sure yet?"
⚙️ Checklist Before Booking First Appt
Intake form received and reviewed PracticeQ
Insurance verified OR self-pay confirmed GHL
Therapist match confirmed and available
If any of the above missing → do not book yet; explain next step to client
💬 Booking the Appointment
"I have [Therapist Name] available on [day] at [time] — does that work for you?"
"Would you prefer mornings, afternoons, or evenings?"
"Will this be in-person at our Coral Gables office, or telehealth?"
[Cancellation notice]: "Just a quick heads up — we do have a 24-hour cancellation policy. If something comes up, please let us know with as much notice as possible."
⚙️ Booking in PracticeQ
Open therapist's calendar in PracticeQ PracticeQ
Confirm appointment type: individual, couples, telehealth, in-person
Book the slot; confirm it shows as "Confirmed" not "Pending"
Move pipeline stage to "First Session Confirmed" if applicable GHL
💬 Confirming
"You're all set! I've got you booked with [Therapist Name] on [day], [date] at [time]. You'll receive a confirmation text and email shortly."
[Telehealth]: "For your telehealth session, you'll receive a video link in your confirmation email. Please test it a few minutes before your appointment time."
[In-person]: "Our office is at [address] in Coral Gables. There's parking available [details]. Please arrive 5–10 minutes early for your first visit."
⚙️ Post-Booking
GHL automation fires: confirmation SMS + email GHL
24-hour reminder will fire automatically — confirm automation is active
Add note: "Booked [date/time] with [therapist]. Client confirmed." GHL

Re-Engagement / Follow-Up Call

Goal: Reconnect warmly, understand what's holding them back, remove barriers

Check GHL history before calling — how many attempts have been made? What was the last interaction?
💬 Opening Scripts
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Love Discovery Institute. I wanted to follow up from when we spoke [last week / a few days ago] — just checking in to see how things are going."
"Hi [Name], I noticed you filled out our form a little while back and I wanted to reach out personally to see if you had any questions or if there's anything I can help with."
Voicemail: "Hi [Name], this is [Name] at Love Discovery Institute — [phone]. We'd love to connect with you when you have a moment. There's no pressure, we just want to make sure you have everything you need. Talk soon!"
🔍 Explore Barriers
"Is there anything that's made it hard to move forward — scheduling, cost, or just feeling uncertain?"
"What would be most helpful for you right now?"
⚙️ Internally
Check GHL: note from previous calls, what was promised, what stage they're in GHL
Check: did they complete the intake form? Was insurance verified?
Max attempts before soft close: 3 outbound calls + 2 texts with no response
After 3 unanswered: move to "Recent Drops" in pipeline; add note
If they re-engage: pick up from where they left off in the pipeline — don't restart from scratch
Log outcome of every attempt in GHL — even voicemails GHL

Billing / Invoice Question

Goal: Answer what you can from GHL tags, escalate anything complex to the billing team

💬 Scripts by Situation
[Co-pay question]: "Let me check your account… I see you have a co-pay of $[X] per session. Does that match what you expected?"
[Unexpected bill]: "I'm sorry for the confusion — let me pull up your account and take a look. I want to make sure we get this sorted out for you."

⚙️ GHL Billing Tag Reference

Tag: unmet deductible → In-network but deductible not met; client pays contracted rate
Tag: +2 therapist → Seeing multiple LDI providers; flag for billing coordination
If disputing a charge → escalate immediately; do not negotiate yourself
Log all billing calls with full detail in GHL notes GHL

Client in Distress / Crisis

Goal: Stay calm, listen first, connect to the right support — never handle alone

If there is any immediate safety concern, direct the caller to 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911. Do not put them on hold. Do not leave them alone on the line.
💬 De-escalation Scripts
"I hear you. I'm right here. Thank you for calling us."
"You did the right thing by reaching out. Can you tell me a little about what's going on?"
[If safety concern]: "I want to make sure you're safe. Are you somewhere safe right now?"
[If immediate danger]: "I want to connect you with someone who can help right now. The crisis line is 988 — they're available 24/7 and are really trained for exactly this. Can I stay on with you for a moment?"
[If urgent but not immediate]: "I hear how hard things are right now. Let me see if one of our therapists is available today or first thing tomorrow. Can you stay on the line with me for just a moment?"
⚙️ Internally — Crisis Protocol
Stay calm. Speak slowly and warmly.
Do NOT put them on hold or transfer without explanation
Immediately notify a supervisor or therapist via internal chat — wave someone over if in-office
If immediate safety risk: do not end the call; provide 988 / 911
If urgent but stable: check for same-day or next-day availability with a therapist
Document the call in full detail after — time, what was said, what action was taken GHL
Debrief with your supervisor after handling a crisis call

By Therapy Type

What brings you to therapy at this point in your life?
Open-ended; let them set the frame
How long have you been feeling this way?
Gauges chronicity vs. acute trigger
Have you been in therapy before? What was helpful or not helpful?
Sets realistic expectations; informs matching
Are there specific goals you'd like to work toward?
Helps therapist alignment
Do you have any preferences for your therapist — gender, background, approach?
Comfort and fit drive engagement
💑 Couples Therapy
Are both of you interested in coming to therapy, or is one of you more uncertain?
Reluctance is common and okay — normalize it
Can you tell me a little about what's been going on between the two of you?
Starts the narrative; stay neutral
Have you been in couples therapy before?
If yes: what worked? what didn't?
What would a successful outcome look like for both of you?
Helps therapist understand whether they want to repair or process a transition
Are both partners comfortable with in-person, or does telehealth work better?
Scheduling logistics for two people
👨‍👩‍👧 Family / Child
How old is your child, and what's been going on for them?
Age guides therapist selection and modality
Is this something you'd like to address in family sessions, or individual sessions for your child?
Clarifies structure before matching
Have there been any major changes at home recently — school, move, loss, divorce?
Context for presenting concerns
Does your child know they'll be coming to therapy? How are they feeling about it?
Helps therapist prepare for a reluctant child

Objections are not rejections — they're questions in disguise. Stay curious, stay warm, and don't push.
Common Objections & Responses
"I need to think about it."
"Absolutely — there's no pressure at all. Can I ask what you're weighing? Sometimes I can help answer a specific question."
If they won't share: "No worries. I'll send you our info and you can reach out whenever you're ready."
"I'm not sure therapy will help me."
"That's a really common concern. The first session is really just a conversation — there's no commitment, and our therapists are great at helping you figure out if it's the right fit."
Normalize ambivalence; don't oversell.
"It's too expensive."
"I hear you — cost is a real consideration. Have you had a chance to check your insurance benefits? We may be able to work with your plan. And if not, I'm happy to go over our self-pay rate and payment options."
Explore insurance first; self-pay as backup.
"I tried therapy before and it didn't help."
"I'm really sorry to hear that. Can I ask what that experience was like? It really does matter who you work with, and we take matching very seriously here."
Shows you're listening; opens a door to a better match.
"I don't have time."
"I totally understand — schedules are tough. We do have early morning, evening, and telehealth options that might be easier to fit in. Would any of those work?"
"My partner doesn't want to come."
"That's really common — a lot of people feel hesitant at first. You could even start with individual sessions and see if that opens the door. Or some couples start with a consultation to just get a feel for it."
Don't pressure; offer an individual path as an entry point.
"Do you take [Insurance Plan]?"
"That's a great question — I want to give you accurate information, so let me have our billing team verify your specific benefits. Can I grab your plan info really quickly?"
Never guess or confirm coverage verbally.
"I'll just look online / find someone else."
"Of course — finding the right fit is the most important thing. If you'd like, I can tell you a bit more about our therapists and approach, and you can decide from there. No pressure either way."
Don't chase. Plant a seed; leave the door open.

CP1 — Greeting & Call Identification

💬 Script
"Hello, thank you for calling Love Discovery. This is [name] speaking. How can I assist you today?"
Listen immediately for tone and urgency — this tells you the call type before you even ask.
⚙️ Internal
Identify call type: New Client / Returning Client / Referral / Other
If uncertain — ask: "Are you reaching out for yourself or on behalf of someone else?"
⚠ Never assume. Determine intent in the first 30 seconds.
Lean In: Assume they are interested and ready unless they say otherwise. Answer with energy and warmth — your tone sets the entire call.

CP2 — Identify Service & Understand Needs

💬 Script
"Could you tell me a bit more about what you're looking for? Are you seeking support for yourself, or are you looking to bring someone else in — like a partner or a child?"
"What's been going on for you lately that brought you to reach out today?"
If they mention a modality (CBT, EMDR): "That's great — let me tell you how we work with that. First, let me understand a little more about what you're experiencing…"
⚙️ Internal
Classify: Individual / Couples / Minors / Family
Note presenting concerns (anxiety, trauma, relationship issues, etc.)
Use client's exact words back to them — this builds immediate rapport
Ask age if minors are involved

CP3 — Educate on Service

💬 Individual
"The first session is about 50 minutes. Sessions are typically weekly, but you can discuss the right cadence with your therapist."
💬 Couples
"The first couples session is a full two hours — this gives both partners time to share their perspective and discuss goals together with the therapist."
⚠ Couples = self-pay only. Do not mention insurance for couples.
💬 Minors
"For younger children, we start with the parents to learn about what's going on. The therapist will then spend time with the child."
"For teens, parents can join the first 15–25 minutes, or the session can be just with your teen — whatever feels most comfortable."

CP4 — Location & Scheduling Preference

💬 Script — Virtual First (always lead here)
"Many of our clients prefer virtual sessions from the comfort of their own home for enhanced privacy and convenience. Of course, we also offer in-person sessions at our Coral Gables office. Which option would you prefer?"
If they say in-person: Later in the call — "I do have a therapist in mind who I think would be a great fit for you, and they work virtually. Would you be open to that if the match is right?"
"When would be a good time for you? Morning, afternoon, or evenings?"
⚙️ Internal
⚠ ALWAYS lead with virtual — this is our priority service
Note AM / PM / Evening preference for matching
For couples: "What might work best for both you and your partner?"
Note flexibility level — some clients are rigid on time, others flexible

CP5 — Two Levels of Therapists

Think of this like presenting two types of wine — aged (premium expertise) vs. not-as-aged (equally great, more accessible). Always give the caller a choice based on what they can invest.
💬 Introduce Both Levels
"We offer two levels of therapists. Our Experts have extensive experience and specialized training — their rate is $400 per session. Our Qualified therapists provide excellent, research-backed care at a more accessible rate, typically $150–$200 per session, and they also accept insurance."
"Would you prefer exploring our qualified therapists in the $150–$200 range, or are you interested in our expert-level therapists?"
Final affordability check: "Are you comfortable with the $150–$200 range, or do you need to stay closer to the lower end?"
⚙️ Internal
Experts ($400): Dr. Pataky, Dr. Riv, Dr. Bacheline Joseph
Qualified ($150–$200): All other therapists
⚠ Only qualified therapists accept insurance
Explore budget depth — don't assume. Ask directly.

Leaning In vs. Leaning Out

✅ Lean In: Listen actively, assume interest, lead with solutions, take action ("Let's get that scheduled right now"), stay warm and direct.

❌ Lean Out: Over-apologizing, hesitating, saying "I'm not sure", introducing doubt with phrases like "It might be hard to find a time", letting silence fill without direction.
